THE JOB SEARCH TRACKER / TJS JOB SAVE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026

TJS Job Save is a browser extension that helps users extract information from job listings and send it to a Job Search Tracker website chosen by the user.

Information processed

When you open the extension on a job listing, it reads information from the active tab, such as the job title, employer, location, posting date, job description, language requirements, and page URL. You can review and edit the extracted details before saving.

How information is used

The extension uses this information solely to prepare a job record and open your chosen Job Search Tracker website with that record for import. The information is included in the destination page's URL fragment; it is not sent to an extension developer-operated server by the extension. However, the destination website, its browser scripts, and browser history may have access to the fragment. Do not include sensitive personal information in job notes.

Local storage

The extension stores your chosen tracker website URL in Chrome's local extension storage so you do not need to enter it again. It does not independently store a history of captured job postings.

Cloud synchronization

If you enable cloud synchronization in your Job Search Tracker website, saved job records may be stored by that website's configured cloud provider (for example, Supabase), subject to the website's settings and access controls. Cloud synchronization is separate from the extension.

Sharing and advertising

The extension does not sell job listing data, use it for advertising, or run analytics or advertising trackers. Information is transferred only to the tracker website you specify when you choose to save a job.

Permissions

The activeTab and scripting permissions allow the extension to extract details from the page you are viewing after you open the extension. The storage permission remembers the tracker website address.

Retention and control

You can remove the saved tracker URL by clearing the extension's local storage or uninstalling the extension. To delete job records imported into your tracker, use the tracker website's deletion features and, if applicable, its cloud account settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the contact email listed on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.

This policy should be updated if the extension's functionality or data handling changes.
